How it works

  1. Collect. Every night Keelwater's feedback is collected from calls, tickets and sessions.
  2. Clean. What customers said is kept, with the line before it as context that is never quoted, and idle session time is cut.
  3. Judge relevance. A small fast model decides what is product signal and what is noise, and a person checks a weekly sample of 40 dropped items.
  4. Extract and check. A model pulls out the problem and the customer's exact words, and code checks the words really appear in the source.
  5. Group and rank. Similar problems are grouped, matched to the roadmap and ranked with a formula the PM can read.
  6. Brief and review. The top ones get a cited brief, three judges from another model family check it, and a PM accepts, merges, rejects, parks or sends it to another squad.
