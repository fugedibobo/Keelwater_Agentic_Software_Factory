Seven stages, five human gates: agents read, draft and check; people decide.

  • The factory is a delegation meta-harness: it routes work to people and to coding tools, and passes the human gates.
  • Its operating rules, in docs/delegation.md, set task classes, routing, review by the other model family, and escalation.
  • Owning the coding harness is not required.

The platform contract

An intent document plus a factory job, checked against a versioned JSON Schema (draft v1) that both teams own. The job carries references and de-identified text; quotes and account names stay inside the company's VPC.

Pilot one squad for 90 days against a control; the digest goes first.

  • The VP Product sponsors it and a pilot squad volunteers.
  • The digest removes status reporting before PMs label anything.
  • My ask is one engineer for 90 days. Without one, day 90 covers three squads, not five.

Adoption without authority

  • Labelling happens in the Monday review, where the board replaces the weekly feedback meeting.
  • Labelling takes a half-day at day 30, then about 30 minutes a week.
  • Three metrics: weekly active PMs, the share of decisions with a written rationale, and the share of customer-visible changes flagged.

  • Coding speed alone cannot do it. In a study of 40 product managers, a 40% productivity gain moved time to market about 5% FOUND.
  • In 90 days, status work falls from a third of PM time to under a tenth and feedback reaches a decision in days. By month 18, lead time is about a fifth lower INFERRED.

The short account

  1. What was used. Claude did most of the work. An Opus session planned, dispatched work orders and merged. Sonnet subagents built 11 of the 12 done or partial work orders (92%), checked every pull request and ran the live steps. Haiku read and searched. Codex was the planned builder but built only one offline generator.
  2. Where I trusted it, and where I overrode it. I trusted mechanical work once the tests ran and a separate check passed. I overrode four AI recommendations: open-weight models instead of a paid frontier API, a public repo, a one-day demo build, and keeping a decision model the AI had dismissed.
  3. What AI got wrong, and how it was caught. The AI put a closed-weight, US-hosted decision model into production triage. Research found it cannot run inside a private network, so I replaced it. A worker's report also said 5 links where the PDF had 4. An independent check caught it, but the first check had passed the same commit, and the issue was reopened.
