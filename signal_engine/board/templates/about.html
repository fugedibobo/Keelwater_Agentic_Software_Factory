About this board

This is the weekly workspace a product manager would get from the Signal Engine: a ranked list of problems customers keep raising, each backed by their exact words. Here it runs on invented data from 30 fictional insurers.

What is real and what is simulated

  • Real: the code, the AI models, the checks and the audit trail. Code verifies every quote against its source, and every model call is logged.
  • Simulated: the customers, calls and tickets. In the static snapshot the PM buttons are switched off.

How one piece of feedback becomes an opportunity

  1. Collect. Calls, tickets and sessions arrive each night.
  2. Filter. A fast model drops small talk, and people sample what it dropped.
  3. Extract. A model pulls out the problem and the customer's words. Code checks the words are really there.
  4. Group and rank. Similar problems merge, and a readable formula orders them.
  5. Brief and decide. The top ten get a cited brief, three AI judges check it, and a PM decides.

What each tab is for

  • 1 Opportunities: the ranked list, with evidence and the PM's actions.
  • 2 Handoffs: the build brief drafted for each accepted item.
  • 3 Pipeline: what each stage kept, dropped and cost.
  • 4 Evals: how the run scored against the planted answers. Several checks fail, and the tab says so.
  • 5 Audit: every decision and model call, in a log that shows tampering.
  • 6 How it works: the architecture diagrams.

Where to start

Open 1 Opportunities and read the top item's quotes. Hover over or tab to the small i marks to see what each number means. Then look at 4 Evals.
